Privacy Policy

YouTube MCP Manager · last updated 14 October 2026

YouTube MCP Manager is a private tool that runs only on its owner's computer. It is used by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels. It is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

With the account holder's permission, given through Google OAuth, the app accesses:

  • the account's email address;
  • channel and video information, playlists and YouTube Analytics reports;
  • public YouTube data such as channel statistics, video details and public comments.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show information to the owner and to carry out actions the owner approves.
  • OAuth tokens are stored in the operating system's credential vault on the owner's computer.
  • Account and channel identifiers are stored in a local database on the same computer.
  • No data is sent to any server operated by the app. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections or through Google's security settings.
  • Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored token and identifiers from the owner's computer.
